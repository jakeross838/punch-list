Support

Punch List keeps your running list of work on each job, with the photos, the companies doing the work, and what is ready.

In the app

Open Me, then Help, for answers to the common questions. Type what you want to do and Claude says how, from the app's own help.

Write to us

Email jake@rossbuilt.com. Say which company you are with and what you were doing, and add a screenshot if you can.

Your account and your data

Delete your account in the app, under Me, Delete My Account. An admin can download everything the company has kept, under Me, Export Everything. The Privacy Policy says what is kept and why, and the Terms of Use say how Punch List may be used.
