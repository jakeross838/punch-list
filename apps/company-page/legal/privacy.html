Privacy Policy

Punch List keeps a builder's running list of work on each job: the items, their photos and marks, the companies doing the work, and who did what. This page says what the app keeps, why, who else handles it for us, and how to have it deleted. Punch List is made by Jake Ross ("we"). Questions go to jake@rossbuilt.com.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, your email, and a phone number if your company's admin adds one. Your company's name and logo.
  • Your jobs: each job's name and address, its locations and plans, the items with their photos, marks, checklists and comments, the visits on the schedule, the lists sent, and the history of who changed what and when.
  • The people your company works with: the companies on your jobs, and the names, emails and phone numbers your team types in for their people so the lists reach them. For a house's owner with their own page: what they add there, and their sign off.
  • Your phone: if you turn on notifications, a code from Apple or Google that lets us send them to your phone. The app also keeps your company's work on the phone so it works with no signal. Signing out clears it.

We do not keep your location, your contacts, or anything about how you use other apps.

How it is used

Only to run Punch List for your company: to keep your phone and your team's in step, to show each company its own list and each owner their page through private links, to send sign in codes and notifications, and to answer when you ask Claude for help. We do not sell it, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who sees it

  • Your company: everyone at your company sees the jobs their role allows. An admin decides who is at the company and what each person can do, under People.
  • The companies on your jobs: each company sees only its own list, through the private link you send it, until you take the link back.
  • A house's owner: only their own page, through the link you give them, until you take it back.
  • A Job Station: whoever is at a screen you open it on sees that job's board.

Anyone who has one of these links can open it, so send each one only to the people it is for.

Claude

When you ask Claude to do something, such as fill in an item from its photo, read the plans or a list, write down what you say, find the same work written twice, write an update or a note, or put a company's list in Spanish, the photo or the words it needs go to Anthropic, which makes Claude, for the answer. Anthropic's commercial terms do not let it train its models on what we send. Claude can get things wrong, so the app shows what it found for you to check.

Who else handles it for us

These companies run parts of Punch List, and handle your data only to do that:

  • Supabase: the database, sign in, the photos, and the email with your sign in code. The data is kept in Canada.
  • PowerSync: keeping each phone in step with the database, in the United States.
  • Expo: the companies', owners' and station pages, and passing notifications on to Apple and Google.
  • Apple and Google: notifications to phones, and Sign in with Apple or Google if you use them.
  • Anthropic: Claude, as above.
  • Resend: email the app sends from its own address, such as invitations, once the app sends them.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your company's data is kept while your company uses Punch List. In the app, Me, Delete My Account takes your sign in away for good; what you did stays with your company under Deleted Account. When the last person at a company deletes their account, the company and everything it kept go too, photos included. An admin can download everything at any time under Me, Export Everything. To have your company's data deleted another way, or for a copy, email jake@rossbuilt.com. Copies in our providers' backups go as those backups are replaced.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels encrypted. The database lets each person see only their own company, as their role allows. Photos are kept private, and each is shown through a link that stops working after an hour.

Children

Punch List is for people at work. It is not for children, and we do not knowingly keep anything about anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with its date, and anything important is said in the app.
